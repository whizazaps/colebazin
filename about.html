I cannot take myself seriously

Cole Bazin is an artist based in Vancouver, Canada. His work explores themes of technology, economics, mythos, fantasy and the intersection of digital and physical realities. Through a variety of peotic methodolgies Bazin challenges viewers to reconsider their relationship with the rapidly evolving technological landscape.
