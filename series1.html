The Golem
Oil on canvas
""
2024

The Golem is a collection often of intimate material, an unfinished being in the eyes of god. In this case this construct is built of a collection of cultural ephemera. The kettle nose of the Golem both a refrence to the animated kettle in beauty and the beast but also a nod to Philip Gustons obsession with his studio kettle. Steam comes from the kettle nose, a common trope of rage but also a refrence to steam engines and a turn in industrialization. With all this paired a nod to chucks which pulls the painting back into the realm of personal and plants it in a more modern context.
